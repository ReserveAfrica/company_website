Load the rates you already have

Drop in a supplier's PDF or spreadsheet, or type the rates in. Reserve Studio sets up the rooms, seasons and prices for you to check and approve, for any lodge, camp or operator you work with. Standard and agent rates sit side by side, and only approved rates can be quoted. And if a supplier uses Reserve Desk, you can connect to them so their rates arrive and update on their own.
