Extraordinary trips. Exhausting admin.

Behind every unforgettable stay is a pile of rate sheets, emails and sums. These are the problems lodges and agents tell us about most, and how we fix them.

The problem
Rate information is fragmented
Rates arrive as PDFs, spreadsheets and emails, each in a different format, making it hard to know which version is current.
How we fix it
One place, one way of pricing
Every rate, season and policy kept together and set out the same way, so you always know what you're looking at.
The problem
Manual calculations
Child ages, single supplements, stays that cross seasons, commission. Every quote is a calculation waiting to go wrong.
How we fix it
Prices calculated for you
Prices are worked out from the stored rates, line by line, so you can see exactly how every total adds up.
The problem
One rate doesn't fit every agent
Standard rates, agent rates and special terms for individual partners end up in separate sheets and emails, and it's easy for the wrong rate to reach the wrong person.
How we fix it
Standard, agent and customised rates together
Every type of rate is kept side by side and clearly labelled, so the right rate always goes to the right agent.
The problem
Mistakes reach the client
A wrong rate copied into a sheet, an outdated price carried into a quote, a lodge invoice with the wrong amount that leads the agent to overcharge their client. By the time anyone notices, it has cost money, or a client's trust.
How we fix it
Correct rates, correct prices
When the rates are right, every quote and itinerary is priced correctly, and invoices can be checked before a client is charged.
The problem
AI quotes built on unverified rates
General AI tools can draft a quote in seconds, but they have no reviewed, approved source of rates. The prices may be out of date, or simply invented.
How we fix it
Quotes from approved rates
Every price comes from rates that have been checked and approved, so a fast quote is also a correct one.
The problem
Admin crowds out the real work
Hours go on finding rates, checking prices and rebuilding quotes. That's time that should be spent designing the holiday itself.
How we fix it
More time for great holidays
With rates that are easier to manage and ready to quote from, there's more time to build trips your clients will remember.
The problem
Every year starts from scratch
New dates, new prices – same risks year on year.
How we fix it
Roll forward, change what's different
Start next year from this year's rates, move the dates on and adjust prices in one go.
